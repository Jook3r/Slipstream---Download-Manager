Slipstream

Folders remembered for categories

Videos on web pages often come as streams of small pieces, with picture and sound apart. Slipstream joins them into one MP4 with ffmpeg, a free tool.

ffmpeg

“Get ffmpeg” downloads a ready-made build (gyan.dev on Windows, martin-riedl.de on macOS) into Slipstream’s own folder.

Browser integration

Install the Slipstream extension

  1. Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi: open chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions), turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and choose the extension folder that came with Slipstream.
  2. Firefox: open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox, click Load Temporary Add-on and choose extension/manifest.json.
  3. Click a download link. Slipstream takes it over; hold Alt while clicking to let the browser download it instead.
